01 / THE CONCEPT

Escape the supermarket.
Restore the power.
Survive the Hunter.

123 Market Run!! is built around an asymmetrical survival concept: a group of Survivors must complete objectives inside a supermarket while being hunted.

The core objective is simple — repair and install four fuses to restore power before the Hunter eliminates the team.
