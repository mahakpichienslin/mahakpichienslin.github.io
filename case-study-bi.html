06 / FROM INSIGHT TO ACTION

Turning analysis
into a roadmap.

NOW 0–3 MONTHS

Connect Digital Leads

Link website inquiries with sales records to measure actual lead-to-sale conversion.

NEXT 3–6 MONTHS

Measure Profitability

Add Cost, Gross Profit and Gross Margin to move beyond revenue-only product analysis.

SCALE 6–12 MONTHS

Build Executive BI

Develop a centralized executive dashboard connecting Sales, Product, Customer and Digital performance.
