01 / CONTEXT

Maintenance starts
with the right diagnosis.

Aviation obstruction lighting systems involve multiple electrical components — from power protection and control systems to drivers and lamps.

When a fault occurs, technicians need to identify the failure point before replacing equipment or performing corrective maintenance.

THE CHALLENGE Different faults can produce similar symptoms.

Troubleshooting therefore requires both technical knowledge and a structured inspection process.

02 / THE PROBLEM

Technical knowledge
shouldn't live only in manuals.

01

Complex System

Multiple electrical components can produce similar failure symptoms, making the source of a fault difficult to identify immediately.

02

Field Diagnosis

Technicians need a quick way to move from observed symptoms toward relevant inspection points while working on-site.

03

Knowledge Gap

Maintenance knowledge often exists across technical manuals and experienced personnel instead of one accessible workflow.

03 / THE SOLUTION

Turning troubleshooting
into an interactive workflow.

INTERACTIVE MAINTENANCE TOOL

From symptom
to recommended action.

I designed a browser-based maintenance assistant that converts technical troubleshooting knowledge into a guided diagnostic experience.

Instead of navigating lengthy documentation, technicians can follow structured inspection paths based on the fault they observe.
